# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be someone people want to work with. Assume the other person is competent and acting in
good faith. Criticise the work, never the person.

## What this means in practice

**Technical disagreement is welcome and expected.** This project makes strong claims
about performance and architecture; those claims should be argued with. Bring a
measurement or a concrete case rather than an assertion, and change your mind when
someone else brings a better one.

**Review the code, not the author.** "This allocates in the hot path" is useful. "This is
sloppy" is not, and it makes the next person quieter rather than better.

**Assume varied context.** Contributors differ in experience, first language, hardware,
and time available. Someone unfamiliar with GPU synchronisation is not unintelligent, and
someone whose English is their third language is not being careless.

**Being right is not a licence.** A correct point delivered contemptuously costs more
than it gains.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory remarks, sustained disruption, publishing
someone's private information, and unwelcome attention of any kind.

## Reporting

Report to the maintainers through
[a private security advisory](https://github.com/Topovium/topovium/security/advisories/new),
which is the only private channel this project currently has. Reports are handled
confidentially.

Maintainers may warn, temporarily block, or permanently ban, and will act on reports
about themselves through the same process rather than judging their own case.

## Scope

Applies in this repository, in issues and pull requests, and in any space where someone
is representing Topovium.
